# 实验 E 从 Prometheus 导出的曲线：名字 | PromQL（导出步长 5 秒）。抓取间隔建议 5 秒，见 experiments/observability/
order_rps_by_status    | sum by (status) (rate(http_server_requests_seconds_count{application="mall-app",uri="/api/orders",method="POST"}[30s]))
order_p99              | histogram_quantile(0.99, sum by (le) (rate(http_server_requests_seconds_bucket{application="mall-app",uri="/api/orders",method="POST"}[30s])))
gateway_status         | sum by (status) (rate(http_server_requests_seconds_count{application="mall-gateway"}[30s]))
reserve_server_rps     | sum by (status) (rate(http_server_requests_seconds_count{application="inventory-service",uri="/internal/inventory/reserve"}[30s]))
reserve_server_p99     | histogram_quantile(0.99, sum by (le) (rate(http_server_requests_seconds_bucket{application="inventory-service",uri="/internal/inventory/reserve"}[30s])))
reserve_server_avg     | sum(rate(http_server_requests_seconds_sum{application="inventory-service",uri="/internal/inventory/reserve"}[30s])) / sum(rate(http_server_requests_seconds_count{application="inventory-service",uri="/internal/inventory/reserve"}[30s]))
reserve_client_p99     | histogram_quantile(0.99, sum by (le) (rate(http_client_requests_seconds_bucket{application="mall-app",uri="/internal/inventory/reserve"}[30s])))
reserve_client_rps     | sum by (uri, outcome) (rate(http_client_requests_seconds_count{application="mall-app",uri=~"/internal/inventory/.*"}[30s]))
hikari_active          | max by (application) (hikaricp_connections_active{application=~"mall-app|inventory-service"})
hikari_pending         | max by (application) (hikaricp_connections_pending{application=~"mall-app|inventory-service"})
hikari_max             | max by (application) (hikaricp_connections_max{application=~"mall-app|inventory-service"})
hikari_acquire_avg     | sum by (application) (rate(hikaricp_connections_acquire_seconds_sum{application=~"mall-app|inventory-service"}[30s])) / sum by (application) (rate(hikaricp_connections_acquire_seconds_count{application=~"mall-app|inventory-service"}[30s]))
hikari_timeout         | sum by (application) (rate(hikaricp_connections_timeout_total{application=~"mall-app|inventory-service"}[30s]))
tomcat_busy            | max by (application) (tomcat_threads_busy_threads{application=~"mall-app|inventory-service"})
tomcat_max             | max by (application) (tomcat_threads_config_max_threads{application=~"mall-app|inventory-service"})
bulkhead_available     | min by (name) (resilience4j_bulkhead_available_concurrent_calls{application="mall-app"})
cb_state               | max by (name, state) (resilience4j_circuitbreaker_state{application="mall-app"})
cb_not_permitted       | sum by (name) (rate(resilience4j_circuitbreaker_not_permitted_calls_total{application="mall-app"}[30s]))
cpu                    | max by (application) (process_cpu_usage{application=~"mall-app|inventory-service|mall-gateway"})
